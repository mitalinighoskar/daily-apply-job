Daily job plan

Targets: 4 applications a day on weekdays, about 18–20 a week, plus 2 recruiter or HR emails a day. Progress is saved in this browser only, one record per day.

These buttons open live searches on each site, filtered to the posting age you choose and to mid-entry experience where the site allows it. The app cannot list jobs itself. Indeed’s longest filter is 14 days, so its “30 days” button uses 14. Skip anything that looks older than a month or already has hundreds of applicants.

Only add numbers you can back up in an interview. Replace anything in [brackets] before sending.
